Moose Backup

Moose Backup is a private backup utility operated by its account owner for their own application recovery. It uploads recovery archives to the owner’s Google Drive, downloads them to verify recovery, and manages the approved backup retention policy.

Google Drive access is requested only from the owner. The utility is not offered as a public sign-up service.

For support, use the support email displayed on the Moose Backup Google consent screen.
